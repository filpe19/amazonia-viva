Clouds, viewing angle and overpass timing affect counts. A month with no detections may lack useful observations.
MODIS and VIIRS differ in resolution and sensitivity. VIIRS can detect smaller and more numerous thermal sources.
Terra and Aqua overpasses have shifted earlier in recent years, affecting afternoon detection counts.
The imported MODIS archive contains differing version values between years (6.2, 6.03, 61.03), potentially introducing discontinuities.
Availability of processed overpass granules has not been audited; only months present in the archives have been checked.
Municipal aggregates use local Parquet subsets. Small counts make percentiles and ratios unstable.
Harmonization pipeline
Steps needed to evaluate a statistically validated MODIS–VIIRS series.
1

Import annual FIRMS country archives
Complete

Streamed ingestion; source URL, Last-Modified and SHA-256 retained.

2

Clip to official IBGE boundaries
Complete

Point-in-polygon with detailed IBGE boundaries: Brazil, Pará and its 144 municipalities.

3

Common temporal aggregation
Complete

UTC day × satellite × day/night × type × confidence; derived monthly values.

4

Common spatial aggregation
Complete

Regular monthly grid: 0.5° for Pará and 0.1° for municipalities, using local Parquet subsets.

5

Coverage and version audit
Partial

Months and file versions audited; granule availability and clouds remain unaudited.

6

Compare overlapping years
Partial

MODIS × NOAA-20 (2021–2025) and S-NPP (2023–2024). Municipal grid: 0.1°. Extend S-NPP history in future work.

7

Sensitivity analysis
Pending

Confidence, presumed vegetation type, day/night, overpass time and Terra/Aqua orbital drift.

8

Statistical evaluation of fusion
Pending

Operational validation remains pending; the existing harmonized series is experimental.
