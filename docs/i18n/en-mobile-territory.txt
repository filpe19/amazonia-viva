What evidence supports the analysis?

NASA FIRMS observations and archives; NASA POWER environmental context; IBGE boundaries and census; official territorial sources distributed through Censipam.

Check source versions, acquisition times, missing observations and the distinction between measured counts and converted estimates. Correlation and proximity do not establish causality or confirmed damage.

Scientific limitations
Heat detections are thermal-anomaly pixels at overpass time. They do not identify individual fires or measure burned area.
Clouds, dense smoke, overpass timing and viewing geometry can prevent detection. No detections does not establish absence of fire.
MODIS (1 km) and VIIRS (375 m) have different sensitivities. Original counts remain separate.
Historical comparisons use the same instrument on both sides. Near-real-time and standard processing may differ slightly.
Terra and Aqua orbital drift has changed overpass times and expected detection counts in recent years.
Meteorology comes from NASA POWER gridded reanalysis, with a delay of days. These are not local measurements.
No fire-spread model has been applied and no forecast is integrated.
The population shown is the territory's census total, not an estimate of affected people.
