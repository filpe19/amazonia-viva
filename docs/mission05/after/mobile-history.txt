How uncertain is the conversion?

Leave-one-year-out evaluation on active months in the overlap period. Errors are absolute percentages of the observed VIIRS count.

The P90 is an error threshold exceeded by the worst 10% of evaluated months, not a 90% accuracy score. The evaluation uses ≥30 detections in both products. Small-count months are not represented by these error statistics.
